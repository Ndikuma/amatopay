Protection timeline

When payment funds become available

1Payer alias verifiedAmatoPay verifies that the payer alias exists and is active.
2Payment authenticatedThe payer approves the RTP through their financial institution and receives a six-digit release code.
3Funds protectedThe completed payment is held separately while the order or service is fulfilled.
4Delivery reviewedPayer confirms with secure code, or submits alias + proof for AmatoPay Operations review.
5Funds releasedOnly secure-code confirmation or completed operations review can move net funds into settlement.
